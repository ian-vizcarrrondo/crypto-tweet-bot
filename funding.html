How to read funding rates

Positive rate — longs pay shorts. Market is bullish/overleveraged long. High positive rates can signal a local top.
Negative rate — shorts pay longs. Market is bearish/overleveraged short. Can signal capitulation or a squeeze setup.
Near zero (±0.01%) — balanced market. No strong directional bias in futures. Often seen in ranging markets.
